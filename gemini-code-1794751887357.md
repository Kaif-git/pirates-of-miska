# Project Miska: Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. High-Level Vision & Core Pillars
**Genre:** Turn-Based Naval Roguelike / Crew & Ship Simulation  
**Inspirations:** *Caves of Qud*, *Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead (CDDA)*, *Dwarf Fortress*, *One Piece*, *RuneScape*  
**Perspective:** 2D Tile / ASCII Grid (Unified Overworld & Tactical Ship Combat)  

### Core Pillars
1. **Systemic Emergence Over Scripted Events:** Ship components, crew traits, wind physics, and magic systems interact dynamically. Igniting an enemy's powder magazine with Fire Floren or knocking an Air user off a railing into deep water resolves through systemic rules, not cutscenes.
2. **Direct Captain, Autonomous Crew:** You control your Captain tile-by-tile like *Caves of Qud*, while your crew operates via *Dwarf Fortress*-style job priorities and high-level tactical commands.
3. **Asymmetric Naval Warfare:** Win encounters through broadside attrition, boarding actions, night-time stealth sabotage, or multi-ship fleet maneuvers.
4. **Lore-Driven Progression:** Power is tied to Miska's socio-political reality—bounties, Marine standing, proximity to metals, behavioral conviction, and the magnetic pull of **The Wall**.

---

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Port & Shipyard (Menu + Blueprint Grid):**
   * Repair structural hull damage, expand your ship's 2D grid, and install functional rooms/weapons.
   * Trade regional goods based on island supply and demand.
   * Turn in bounties, ransom prisoners, recruit tavern sailors, and purchase equipment or black-market intel.
2. **Overworld Sailing (Turn-Based Sea Grid):**
   * Navigate procedurally generated ocean sectors affected by wind currents, fog banks, and storm fronts.
   * Encounter Marine patrols, rival pirate captains, merchant convoys, floating wrecks, and treasure coordinates.
   * Manage crew rations, fresh water, grog/morale, and onboard maintenance.
3. **Tactical Encounters (Unified Grid Combat):**
   * Seamless transition into tactical turns when engaging an enemy vessel or hazard.
   * Utilize cannons, ship maneuvering, stealth swimming, boarding planks, and **Fusion / Floren** abilities.
4. **Aftermath & Scaling:**
   * Loot cargo, salvage rare metals, extract **Sharya Seeds** from fallen Floren masters, and capture surviving enemies as recruits, ransom targets, or forced labor.

---

## 3. World Standing & Progression Metrics
Instead of traditional XP levels, your standing in Miska is governed by three global variables and *RuneScape*-style skill mastery.

### The Three Global Metrics
| Metric | Range | Mechanical Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Notoriety (Infamy)** | `0` to `1000` | Measures fear. High Notoriety causes weak merchants and low-tier pirates to flee on sight, while attracting elite bounty hunters and Marine task forces. |
| **Marine Standing** | `-100` to `+100` | **Positive:** Access to Marine ports, cheap repairs, legal Letters of Marque, and official bounty contracts.<br>**Negative:** Locked out of legal ports, forced to use Black Market coves, triggers Marine blockades. |
| **Individual Bounties** | `0` to `∞` Berries/Marks | Assigned individually to your Captain and named officers based on crimes, Marine kills, and forbidden magic usage (e.g., *Decay Body Floren*). High-bounty officers attract rival named captains. |

### Grounded Skills (Level 1–99 via Use)
* **Gunnery:** Cannon reload speed, accuracy, and critical hit chance against ship components.
* **Navigation:** Overworld movement efficiency, wind reading, and storm survival.
* **Swordsmanship / Brawling:** Melee accuracy, parry chance, and damage during boarding skirmishes.
* **Command:** Maximum fleet size (vassal ships), crew morale stability, and prisoner intimidation.
* **Stealth:** Vision cone avoidance during night raids, silent swimming, and sabotage speed.
* **Carpentry / Engineering:** Speed of mid-sea hull patching and shipyard upgrade discounts.

## 4. Ship Blueprints & Modular Building
Ships are constructed and customized on a **2D Tile Grid** (ranging from a `5x10` Starter Sloop to a `10x24` War Galleon) using an ASCII/Tile Blueprint interface.

### Hull Materials
* **Pine / Oak:** Cheap, standard weight, vulnerable to fire and heavy cannon fire.
* **Ironwood:** High structural HP, resistant to splintering.
* **Copper-Plated Hull:** Immune to fire/magma abilities and conducts Copper Fusion signals across the entire vessel.
* **Titanite-Reinforced Hull:** End-game armor; drastically reduces incoming cannon damage.

### Functional Tiles & Room Zones
* **Helm / Wheel:** Must be manned by the Captain or a Navigator to turn the ship and adjust sail speed.
* **Cannons & Deck Weapons:**
  * *Standard Cannon:* Heavy hull and crew damage.
  * *Chain-Shot Launcher:* Shreds enemy sails to immobilize them for boarding.
  * *Swivel Gun:* Anti-personnel grapeshot for defending narrow deck chokepoints.
  * *Harpoon Winch:* Pulls an adjacent ship flush against your hull.
* **Powder Magazine:** Stores blackpowder. Placing it near cannons speeds up crew reloading, **but** any fire/combustion hit on this tile triggers a catastrophic chain-reaction explosion.
* **The Brig:** Houses captured prisoners. Higher tile count increases capacity; Obsidian-sealed bars reduce escape/mutiny risk to zero.
* **Galley & Infirmary:** Passively regenerates crew morale and heals non-permanent injuries between encounters.
* **Captain's Quarters:** Increases maximum vassal fleet slots and diplomatic/ransom payouts.

## 5. Crew Simulation & Prisoner Management
Every NPC is generated with individual stats, behavioral traits, and job priorities (*Dwarf Fortress* style).

### Crew Roles & Job Priorities
Even low-stat NPCs serve vital logistical roles on a living ship:
* **Swabbing & Hauling:** Cleans filth (preventing disease outbreaks), puts out fires, and physically carries gunpowder/shot from the hold to the cannons.
* **Rigging & Sails:** Adjusts sails to maximize wind speed.
* **Gunnery:** Loads, aims, and fires assigned cannons.
* **Damage Control:** Patches hull leaks and bails out rising seawater.
* **Boarding / Marine Guard:** Equips melee weapons and defends deck chokepoints or jumps onto enemy vessels.

### Handling Prisoners
When an enemy crew surrenders or is incapacitated, you decide their fate:
1. **Recruit:** Persuade or bribe them to join your crew. Loyalty grows over time if paid and fed well.
2. **Turn In / Ransom:** Deliver pirates to Marine ports for full bounty payouts, or ransom Marine officers back to the Navy for gold (lowers Marine Standing).
3. **Forced Labor (Enslavement):** Put prisoners in chains to row, bail water, or haul cargo for zero wages. Generates a constant **Mutiny/Escape Meter**—if your crew is weakened in battle or a storm, prisoners will break free and attack from behind.

## 6. Tactical Combat: Unified Grid & Victory Paths
Combat uses a **Relative Grid System** (the player's ship remains anchored in coordinate space while the ocean and enemy ships shift around it, keeping onboard crew pathfinding fast and clean).

### Four Ways to Win
1. **Broadside Attrition:** Outmaneuver the enemy using wind angles and pound their **Hull** (causes sinking), **Sails** (immobilizes), or **Rudder** (locks steering).
2. **Boarding & Deck Skirmish:** Pull alongside or use movement abilities (e.g., *Steel Magnet-Leap* or *Air Flight*) to cross onto the enemy grid. Fight tile-by-tile until their Captain falls or surrenders.
3. **Night Stealth Sabotage:** Engage at night when vision cones shrink. Send high-*Stealth* swimmers into the water (or via rowboat) to climb the enemy hull undetected, silence lookouts, and sabotage their **Rudder** or ignite their **Powder Magazine**.
4. **Vassal Fleet Swarm:** Assign trusted officers as Captains of captured ships. Issue real-time fleet orders to flank, pin, or blockade targets.

---

## 7. The Magic System of Miska: Fusion & Floren
Magic is deeply integrated into ship roles, economics, and character behavior. When a high-level user dies, they drop **Resonant Ingots (Fusion)** or crystallized **Sharya Seeds (Floren)**, allowing players to loot and cultivate powers without breaking lore.

### System A: Fusion (Metal Manipulation)
* **Core Mechanic:** Mages bond with metals through proximity, study, and equipping Resonant Ingots. Bonding a second metal requires unlocking a separate **Mental Stance Slot**.
* **Universal Ability (Shard Orbit):** Every Fusion user can break their bonded metal into orbiting shards—launching them as projectiles or contracting them inward to shove enemies off ship railings into the water.

| Metal | Ship & Overworld Utility | Tactical Combat Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron** | Passively repairs wooden/iron hull tiles over time; detects hidden ships via water vibrations. | **Seismic Sense:** Reveals enemies through walls/fog; fires heavy shrapnel. |
| **Copper** | Enables real-time fleet commands across vast distances; planted copper wires track enemy ships on the world map. | **Filament Traps:** Strings invisible razor wire across doorways during boarding. |
| **Steel** | Boosts ship turning speed and mitigates headwind penalties by nudging steel ship fittings. | **Magnet-Leap:** Launches the user across water gaps directly onto steel cannons or armored foes. |
| **Titanite** | Unlocks top-tier hull reinforcements and extends cannon range/ignition speed. | **Structural Reinforce:** Hardens cover tiles mid-fight and shatters enemy armor. |
| **Obsidian** | Seals the Brig (0% escape) and Vault; authenticates high-tier legal/black-market contracts. | **Seal & Sever:** High armor-piercing shards; seals doors shut to trap enemies in burning rooms. |
| **[Classified]** | Endgame government metal; prevents permanent stat loss from fatal wounds. | **Anomalous Severance:** Bypasses all physical defenses; drains vitality. |

### System B: Floren (Purpose & Conviction)
* **Core Mechanic:** Elemental power fueled by **Conviction** (how faithfully the character acts according to their element's psychological nature) and proximity to **The Wall**.
* **Looting Mechanic:** Defeating a Floren user drops a **Sharya Seed** of their active branch, which can be absorbed by a crew member—binding them to that element's behavioral rules.

#### The Six Elements & Behavioral Rules
1. **Fire Floren (Conviction = Aggression & Momentum):**
   * *Scaling:* Grows stronger every consecutive turn spent attacking or advancing without retreating.
   * *Branches:* **Spark** (ignites tiles), **Heat** (raises room temperature until enemy crew passes out), **Lightning** (instant line-of-sight arcs), **Combustion** (detonates gunpowder/ammo), **Absorption** (consumes fire on your own ship to heal/buff).
2. **Water Floren (Scaling = Environmental Moisture):**
   * *Scaling:* Peak power on the open ocean; weakens on dry land unless carrying water casks.
   * *Branches:* **Wave/Current** (pushes ships off course), **Ice/Vapor** (creates ice bridges between ships or scalding steam clouds), **Pressure** (blasts holes in hulls), **Mist/Fog** (blinds enemy gunners), **Fluid/Blood** (heals allies or manipulates enemy blood at high mastery), **Storm Calling** (alters overworld weather).
3. **Earth Floren (Conviction = Stubbornness & Rootedness):**
   * *Scaling:* Gains stacking armor and power for every turn spent standing on the exact same tile.
   * *Branches:* **Stone/Rock**, **Sand/Soil**, **Wood/Root** (rapidly regrows wooden ship planks), **Crystal/Mineral**, **Metal/Ore** (can hijack a Fusion mage's metal shards mid-air), **Magnetism**, **Seismic**.
4. **Air Floren (Conviction = Detachment & Anonymity):**
   * *Scaling:* Scales **inversely** with Notoriety and Bounty. If the user becomes famous, their power fades. Ideal for low-profile stealth operatives.
   * *Branches:* **Wind/Gust**, **Breath/Suffocation** (silent night-raid takedowns), **Sound/Vibration**, **Flight/Levitation** (ignores water gaps), **Void/Stillness** (complete stealth invisibility).
5. **Body Floren (Conviction = Perpetual Hunger & Experimentation):**
   * *Scaling:* Power stagnates if the user stops upgrading gear, mutating, or treating complex wounds.
   * *Branches:* **Healing/Restoration**, **Enhancement/Modification** (mutates crew into superhumans), **Toxicology**, **Disease/Decay** (rots enemy hulls and rations—**triggers immediate `-100` Kill-on-Sight Marine status if witnessed**), **Creature Biology**, **Flora**, **Neurology** (interrogates prisoners without physical marks).
6. **Soul Floren (Conviction = Empathy or Manipulation):**
   * *Scaling:* Grows through social interaction, prisoner interrogation, and morale manipulation.
   * *Branches:* **Emotional Reading**, **Memory** (extracts hidden treasure/map coordinates from prisoners), **Will & Suggestion** (converts prisoners instantly), **Domination** (forces enemies to attack allies; *Warning: Soul-enslaved Floren users suffer a permanent skill ceiling*), **Identity & Persona** (disguises ship/crew alignment).

---

## 8. Endgame: The Wall
Along the far edge of the Miska ocean grid stretches **The Wall**, where the **Sharya spirits** are imprisoned.
* **The Pull:** Every Floren user on your crew experiences stronger visions and higher passive power the closer your fleet sails to The Wall.
* **The Gauntlet:** Guarded by World Government dreadnoughts plated in Titanite, elite Body-Modified Marine units, and Classified-Metal commanders.
* **The Goal:** Breach the blockade, confront the truth of the Void Era, and decide whether to reinforce the seal or awaken the Sharya source.

---

## 9. Minimalist MVP Roadmap (First Playable Build)
To get a fun, playable prototype running quickly before scaling content:

1. **Phase 1: The Static Ship Grid & Movement**
   * Build a `6x12` ASCII/tile player Sloop (`Floor`, `Hull Wall`, `Door`, `Helm`, `Cannon`, `Powder Crate`).
   * Implement *Caves of Qud* turn-based movement for the Captain + 3 autonomous crew members with basic job priorities (`Load Cannon`, `Repair Hull`, `Fight`).
2. **Phase 2: Relative Naval Combat**
   * Spawn 1 enemy Pirate Sloop on an open water grid.
   * Implement wind direction, cannon firing, hull/sail damage, and walking across a boarding plank to fight in melee.
3. **Phase 3: Core Magic Slice (2 Metals + 2 Florens)**
   * Implement **Steel Fusion** (Magnet-Leap + Shard Push) and **Iron Fusion** (Hull Repair + Wall Sense).
   * Implement **Fire Floren** (*Combustion* + Aggression meter) and **Water Floren** (*Mist/Fog* + *Wave Push*).
4. **Phase 4: Port Loop & Bounty Progression**
   * Add 2 island ports with a simple trade screen, ship repair/tile editing, tavern recruitment, and a working **Bounty / Notoriety** tracker.



## 1. Executive Summary & Core Design Philosophy
**Genre:** Systemic Turn-Based Naval Roguelike / Immersive Crew & Ship Simulation  
**Inspirations:** *Caves of Qud*, *Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead (CDDA)*, *Dwarf Fortress*, *One Piece*, *RuneScape*  
**Engine Target:** Godot 4 / Custom Grid Engine (Optimized for low-spec hardware without dedicated GPUs)  

Project Miska combines the depth of open-world rogue-lites with the tactile, systemic simulation of *Dwarf Fortress* and *Caves of Qud*. You play as a rising captain carving out an empire in the archipelago world of **Miska**, where politics, economics, and warfare are entirely governed by two interconnected magic systems: **Fusion** and **Floren**.

---

## 2. Comprehensive Core Loop & Progression Flow

[Port: Tavern, Shipyard, Market, Bounty Board]
│ (Buy blueprints, recruit crew, trade, repair)
▼
[Overworld Sea Grid: Sailing, Weather, Encounters]
│ (Navigate winds, hunt bounties, evade Marine fleets)
▼
[Tactical Engagement: Broadside, Boarding, or Stealth]
│ (Turn-based combat on unified ship tile grids)
▼
[Aftermath: Salvage, Sharya Extraction, Prisoner Processing]
│ (Loot ingots, harvest seeds, recruit/enslave/ransom)
▼
[Notoriety & Bounty Update ➔ Return to Port]


### The Three Operational Phases
1. **The Haven Phase (Ports & Cities):** 
   * Repair structural hull damage, expand hull tiles, upgrade rooms, and trade regional commodities.
   * Manage your crew's pay, food supplies, rum rations, and morale.
   * Turn in captured pirate bounties for Berries/Marks, or bribe Marine officials to lower your local Infamy.
2. **The Voyage Phase (Overworld Sea Grid):**
   * Move tile-by-tile across a procedural ocean map influenced by seasonal wind vectors, fog banks, and storm fronts.
   * Manage consumable resources: **Rations** (starvation causes mutiny), **Fresh Water** (dehydration lowers AP), **Timber/Planks** (required for mid-sea hull patches), and **Grog** (maintains crew spirits during storms).
3. **The Confrontation Phase (Tactical Action):**
   * Instant transition to turn-based grid combat when colliding with hostiles. Every crew member, enemy, and cannon operates on a unified turn queue.

## 3. World Economy, Trade, and Island Specialization
Miska's economy is node-based and regional. Islands produce specific raw materials and suffer deficits in others, creating lucrative trade routes for an enterprising captain.

### Regional Economies & Commodity Matrix
* **Volcanic Calderas (Titanite / Obsidian / Coal):** Produce heavy metals, raw minerals, and volcanic glass. Desperately import fresh agricultural produce and timber.
* **Coral Atolls & Fishing Hubs (Rations / Salt / Pearls):** Abundant food and clean water; high demand for manufactured weapons, blackpowder, and iron nails.
* **Forested Archipelagos (Timber / Resin / Exotic Fruit):** The primary source for ship-building materials and shipwright recruitment.
* **Marine Strongholds (Enforced Trade / Legal Contracts):** High security, strictly regulated goods. Smuggling contraband here yields 300% profit but risks immediate Marine interdiction if searched.

### Smuggling & Contraband Loops
* **Hidden Cargo Holds:** Install false-floor tile upgrades in your ship blueprint to conceal illegal items (Decay body parts, outlawed Soul Floren grimoires, stolen government bullion) from Marine inspection patrols.
* **Bribe & Corrupt:** If intercepted by a Marine patrol on the overworld map, use Copper/Obsidian legal contracts or cold cash to buy your way out—or initiate a surprise combat ambush before they sound the alarm.

---

## 4. Expanded Crew Management, Labor, and Mutiny Systems
Your crew is not a generic health bar; they are named, procedurally generated NPCs with distinct psychological quirks, stats, and skill sets.

### Crew Morale & Mutiny Triggers
* **Morale Factors:** Rations quality, grog availability, unpaid wages, excessive combat casualties, and tyrannical captain behavior (e.g., executing crewmates or using Soul Floren slavery).
* **The Mutiny Spiral:** When crew morale hits `0`, a mutiny event triggers:
  * Armed mutineers seize the lower deck, locking you in the Captain's Quarters.
  * You must fight your way through your own ship tile-by-tile using your Captain's personal combat abilities to restore order or execute the ringleaders.

### Detailed Prisoner Lifecycle
When you disable and board an enemy ship, surviving crew members can be processed in four ways:
1. **Voluntary Recruitment:** Persuade prisoners with high compatibility. They join your crew with neutral loyalty.
2. **Marine Bounty Exchange:** Deliver captured pirate captains to official Marine ports to collect high-value cash bounties and restore minor Marine Standing.
3. **Officer Ransom:** Hold captured Marine officers in the Brig until their government pays a ransom in gold (drops Marine Standing).
4. **Forced Chain-Gang Labor:** Lock prisoners in Brig tiles to row oars or bail water. 
   * *The Danger:* Chained prisoners generate constant escape tension. If your ship takes heavy damage in a storm, they will pick their locks and join the fray against you.

## 5. In-Depth Ship Customization & Architecture
Ships are fully realized tile grids. Upgrading your ship changes its physical topology, creating tactical choke points for boarding and fire defense.

### Expanded Room Modules & Functional Tiles
* **Alchemical Laboratory:** Allows your crew to refine harvested Sharya Seeds, brew healing draughts, or synthesize volatile gunpowder enhancements.
* **Navigator's Astrolabe Deck:** A raised tile platform that grants the Captain or Navigator extended vision range on the overworld map and early storm warnings.
* **Harpoon & Winch Batteries:** Heavy deck-mounted winches used to snag enemy hulls and haul them flush against your ship for instant boarding actions.
* **Brig Cages (Reinforced Iron/Obsidian):** Secure holding cells preventing prisoner escapes.
* **Surgeon’s Table (Infirmary):** Required to heal permanent crew injuries and safely perform Body Floren augmentations without killing the patient.

---

## 6. Advanced Tactical Combat & Environmental Hazards
Combat leverages a rich physics and status interaction model.

### Environmental & Elemental Interactions on the Grid
* **Wind Vectors & Broadside Physics:** Firing cannons downwind increases projectile velocity and range. Firing into the wind reduces accuracy and can blow smoke back onto your own deck, blinding gunners.
* **Fire & Combustion Chains:** If a Fire Floren user casts *Combustion* or an enemy cannon shot hits an unshielded tile adjacent to your **Powder Magazine**, a chain-reaction explosion blows out hull walls and jettisons nearby crew into the sea.
* **Flooding & Bailing:** Hitting an enemy hull below the waterline creates a *Leak Tile*. Water fills adjacent floor tiles each turn. If water reaches the cargo hold or powder room, cargo is ruined and cannons misfire. Crew members must be assigned to *Bail Water* buckets.
* **Drowning Mechanics:** Any character (player, crew, or enemy) knocked onto a deep water tile without swimming gear or flight abilities immediately begins drowning, requiring rescue or resulting in instant death (fatal for Cursed/Relic users and Devil Fruit equivalents).

## 7. Extended Lore, Factions, and Endgame Goals
The world of Miska is shaped by institutional power struggles rooted in its magic systems.

### Major Factions of Miska
1. **The World Government (The Navy):** Backed by Obsidian legal authority, Titanite smiths, and elite Body-Modified Marine super-soldiers. They enforce strict control over trade and hunt unregistered Floren users.
2. **The Free Syndicate (Pirates & Privateers):** A loose coalition of independent captains, smugglers, and outcast Fusion mages operating out of hidden Black Market coves.
3. **The Sharia Cult / Wall Zealots:** Mysterious ascetics who worship the Sharya spirits behind The Wall, seeking to tear down the boundary and flood Miska with raw elemental chaos.

### The Endgame Ascent to The Wall
* As your crew gathers higher-tier Sharya Seeds and master Fusion ingots, their psychological pull toward **The Wall** intensifies.
* Reaching the outer ring requires defeating elite government dreadnoughts and navigating anomalous weather storms where magic behaves unpredictably.
* At The Wall, you face the ultimate choice: reinforce the ancient seals to preserve human civilization, or shatter the barrier to unlock infinite power at the cost of the world's stability.
